House Rules⌄

Check-in & Check-out

Check-in: 3:00 PM
Check-out: 11:00 AM
Early check-in or late check-out may be arranged in advance, subject to availability.

Quiet Hours & Parties

  • Quiet hours are 10:00 PM–8:00 AM.
  • Parties and events are strictly prohibited.
  • Keep music and conversations at a reasonable volume. Sound travels easily between villas.
  • Serious noise violations or unauthorized parties may result in termination of the stay without refund, subject to booking terms and applicable law.

Guests & Visitors

  • Maximum occupancy: 6 guests per villa.
  • Only registered guests may stay overnight. Additional visitors require prior approval.

Swimming Pool

  • Pool hours: 8:00 AM–10:00 PM. No use outside these hours.
  • No glass bottles or glassware in or around the pool.
  • Children must be supervised by an adult at all times. Swimming is at your own risk.
  • Please shower before entering the pool.
  • Dispose of bottles, cans, food containers, and rubbish in the bins after using the pool. Do not leave personal belongings behind.
  • Be considerate of other guests and keep noise levels reasonable.
  • Repeated or serious after-hours violations may result in penalties of up to 5,000 THB, only where disclosed in the booking terms and permitted by the booking platform and applicable law.
  • Additional charges may apply for documented excessive cleaning beyond normal maintenance.

Smoking

No smoking indoors. Smoking is permitted outdoors; dispose of cigarette butts responsibly.

Toilets & Septic System

Do not flush toilet paper, tissues, wipes, or sanitary products. Use the bathroom bins provided. A 1,000 THB maintenance fee applies if a blockage is caused by flushing prohibited items.

Towels & Linens

Pool towels are provided. Please keep bathroom towels indoors and do not remove any towels or linens from the property.

Furniture & Property Care

  • Indoor furniture must stay inside the villa; do not move it outdoors or between villas.
  • Keep outdoor furniture in its designated area and never place furniture in the pool.
  • Please do not rearrange heavy furniture.
  • Report damage promptly. Guests are responsible for damage they cause.
  • Excessive mess may result in additional cleaning charges.
  • Do not feed stray animals.

Keys & Security

Lock doors and windows when leaving. Lost or unreturned keys incur a 500 THB replacement fee.

Pets

Pets are not permitted unless approved in advance.

Electricity & Air Conditioning⌄

Electricity Charges

Electricity is charged based on actual consumption at 7 THB per kWh. We photograph the electricity meter at check-in and check-out. Your total electricity charge is calculated from the difference between the two readings.

How to Keep Electricity Costs Down

Please turn off air conditioners when leaving the villa or when a room is not in use. For example, avoid leaving the lounge air conditioner running overnight while everyone is asleep in their bedrooms.

To cool a warm room quickly, set the air conditioner to 18°C and use the Turbo/Jet function for about 10 minutes. Then turn Turbo/Jet off and adjust the temperature to 22–24°C. This can help maintain comfort without continuously running the unit at a very low setting.

Keep doors and windows closed while the air conditioner is running.

Trash Disposal⌄

The designated trash bins are located in the communal bin area. Please place your trash in these bins. Our staff empty them daily.

Household Supplies⌄

To help you get settled in, we provide an initial supply of toilet paper, paper towels, trash bags, body wash, and shampoo at check-in.

These are starter supplies and are not replenished during your stay. Additional supplies can be purchased at a nearby 7-Eleven or local Thai convenience store.

Cleaning Services⌄

Cleaning during your stay is not included unless previously arranged. Additional cleaning can be scheduled for a fee.

Please contact us for pricing and availability and provide at least 48 hours’ notice to arrange the cleaning team.

Tropical Environment & Wildlife⌄

Koh Phangan is a beautiful tropical island, and its natural environment includes insects, spiders, geckos (small lizards), and other wildlife.

While we do our best to keep the villas clean and minimize unwanted visitors, it is not possible to completely prevent insects and small creatures from occasionally entering the property.

For your convenience, insect spray is provided under the kitchen sink should you need it. Please follow the instructions on the product label.

Keeping doors and windows closed when not in use, especially in the evenings, can help minimize insects entering the villa.

Thank you for your understanding and for embracing island life!

Check-out Instructions⌄

Before departure, please:

  • Take trash to the designated bins.
  • Wash used dishes.
  • Return furniture and other items to their original positions.
  • Turn off lights and air conditioners.
  • Close and lock doors and windows.
  • Return the key to the keybox.

Thank you for staying with us!
